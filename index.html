      NTG CHANGELOG

      Mobile: Responsive landscape controls, joystick dead zone, pointer-safe input, and a full-screen homework layout.

      Co-op: Five-player rooms use pushed position updates while game rules stay local. Link up for a speed/energy boost and bonus coins; nearby teammates can rescue you once per minute.

      Multiplayer Games: Browse open rooms or host School Co-op and Skyline Scramble. Skyline adds spring pads, boost rails, crumble blocks, local powerups, animated drones, and a prism-locked finish gate.

      Movement: Independent sprint stamina, recovery, stronger dash, animated player trails, and dynamic energy colors.

      World: Levels 1-10, longer side-scrolling wings, randomized classes, teachers, reporters, and classroom decor.

      Flow: Current-level retries, explicit door interaction, level completion navigation, and pause/menu controls.

      Challenge: Teachers now fill a three-step Trouble Meter instead of causing instant detention. Choose Easy, Normal, Hard, or unlock Extreme by clearing every level on Hard.

      Progression: Levels unlock in order and can be replayed from the level select menu after they have been cleared.

      Admin: Password-locked local developer tools can unlock progression and abilities, complete objectives, adjust coins and supplies, add helpers, refill stats, and clear trouble.

      Variety: Every generated level now has its own route profile with different length, platform spacing, vertical jumps, hazards, teacher coverage, and reporter speeds. Homework also includes level-specific question variants across every subject, with later levels becoming harder to traverse and solve.
